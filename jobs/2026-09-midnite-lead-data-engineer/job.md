👀 The Role

Title: Lead Data Engineer

Team: Data

Location: UK, remote

Salary: £100,000 - £120,000 (flexibility for exceptional candidate)

We're looking for a Lead Data Engineer to drive the next phase of our data platform strategy at Midnite. This is a hands-on technical leadership role where you'll set direction, own the design and scalability of our data infrastructure, and help build the platform that enables analysts and stakeholders to self-serve.

We're looking for a software-engineering-first data platform leader: someone with strong engineering fundamentals, sound technical judgment, and the ability to mentor others while remaining hands-on.

You'll work across the full data lifecycle from ingestion and modelling to orchestration, monitoring, and analytics enablement. You'll help us make scalable technical decisions, support multi-territory expansion, improve platform reliability, and move the business toward lower-latency insights where it matters most.

Our Tech Stack

Snowflake, Apache Iceberg, Python, Docker, Dagster, dbt, Terraform, AWS, and Omni.

We have a modern stack overall and welcome input from engineering leaders on how we can improve scalability, reliability, and efficiency.

You will:

- Partner with the Head of Data to own the technical strategy for data engineering, ensuring our stack scales with the business.
- Design, maintain, and evolve robust data pipelines and architecture to support reliable analytics and lower-latency opportunities over time.
- Build a platform that enables analysts to self-serve and stakeholders to make better decisions.
- Help shape repeatable architecture for multi-territory expansion.
- Oversee the implementation of data models and frameworks that support analytics and business intelligence.
- Drive engineering best practices across testing, monitoring, version control, automation, and pragmatic design.
- Lead code reviews, raise quality standards, and ensure technical debt is managed proactively.
- Mentor engineers, support career development, and provide lightweight people management while staying hands-on.
- Stay ahead of industry trends, introducing tools and methods that future-proof the data platform without overcomplicating it.

The next Midniter:

- Is a software engineer first who has specialised in data/platform engineering.
- Has strong software engineering fundamentals.
- Has strong Python or equivalent production engineering capability.
- Has SQL capability; Snowflake experience is a bonus, but engineering depth matters more than exact tool match.
- Has data modelling and architecture breadth, and can explain trade-offs clearly.
- Has experience with orchestration frameworks such as Dagster, Airflow, or Prefect.
- Has hands-on experience with modern data warehouses/lakehouse patterns and cloud infrastructure.
- Is comfortable with platform/infrastructure thinking across tools such as dbt, Docker, Terraform, AWS, Iceberg, or similar.
- Is a hands-on leader: data engineer first, people manager second.
- Thrives in a start-up/scale-up environment and brings a pragmatic, impact-focused mindset.
